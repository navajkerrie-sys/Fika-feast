Our story

Fika&Feast by Ashaki is a barbecue restaurant in Nakuru built around one idea: good meat, cooked properly and shared generously. Families, school trips and old regulars fill our tables, and many come back after years away for the plate they remember.

Take a fika. Stay for the feast.
